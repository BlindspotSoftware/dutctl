**Control the devices on your lab bench from anywhere.** dutctl powers, flashes and talks to boards under test from
your laptop or your CI, through an agent on a Raspberry Pi that is wired to them.

## How these docs work

[Get started](get-started/) teaches by doing. The guides [Use dutctl](use/), [Set up agents](setup/) and
[Write modules](develop/) lead you through the tasks of your role, and the icons beside each link name the roles a
page is for. [Reference](reference/) describes dutctl as it is, [Concepts](concepts/) explains why it is so, with the
[design decisions](concepts/decisions/). The pages follow [Diátaxis](https://diataxis.fr); each is a file in the
[repository](https://github.com/BlindspotSoftware/dutctl), which [contributions](project/contributing/) improve.
